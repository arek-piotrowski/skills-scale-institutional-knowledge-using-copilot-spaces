# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Collaborate with QA Leads on test strategy and acceptance criteria
- Work with Technical Leads on architecture and design reviews
- Partner with Product Managers on feature requirements and prioritization
- Report to Project Managers on progress and blockers

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Align with Executive Sponsors on business priorities
- Consult Design/UX Leads on user experience implications
- Work with Technical Leads on feasibility and trade-offs
- Partner with QA Leads on acceptance criteria definition

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Escalate risks and blockers to Executive Sponsors
- Coordinate dependencies with Technical Leads and other teams
- Facilitate communication between all personas
- Track progress with input from QA Leads on quality metrics

---

## QA Lead / Quality Assurance Manager

### Role Summary
QA Leads own quality standards, define and execute test strategies, and validate that acceptance criteria are met before release. They collaborate with product and engineering to ensure features meet quality bar.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and QA approach for features and releases
- Create and maintain automated test suites (unit, integration, end-to-end)
- Validate acceptance criteria and sign off on feature completion
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigations
- Coordinate manual QA and regression testing
- Participate in release readiness reviews

### Goals
- Maintain high quality bar and prevent regressions
- Reduce cycle time for testing and validation
- Provide early visibility into quality risks

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement (acceptance criteria review)
- PR reviews and testing checklists
- Release readiness gates and sign-offs
- Quality metrics and trend reporting

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Work with Developers on test coverage and code quality
- Align with Product Managers on acceptance criteria definition
- Collaborate with Design/UX Leads on usability testing
- Report quality status to Project Managers and Executive Sponsors
- Coordinate with Technical Leads on test infrastructure and automation

---

## Stakeholder / Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Executive Sponsors provide business context, strategic alignment, and decision authority. They ensure projects align with organizational goals and remove business-level blockers.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and strategic direction
- Approve major scope and timeline decisions
- Escalate and resolve business-level blockers
- Review and approve releases and key milestones
- Communicate project status to broader organization

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value
- Maintain alignment with organizational strategy
- Minimize business impact from delays or issues

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and status reviews
- Major decision gates (go/no-go decisions)
- Escalation and incident notifications
- Release announcements

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Receive escalations from Project Managers on blockers and risks
- Approve prioritization decisions made by Product Managers
- Review and sign off on release plans from Project Managers
- Provide strategic guidance to Technical Leads on architectural decisions

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads define technical direction, review system design, and identify technical risks. They guide architectural decisions and mentor developers on best practices.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design standards
- Review technical designs and code for alignment with standards
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Mentor developers and promote engineering excellence
- Collaborate on technology selection and trade-offs
- Advocate for technical debt reduction

### Goals
- Enable scalable, maintainable system architecture
- Reduce technical risk and long-term cost of changes
- Foster a culture of continuous learning and improvement

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review feedback and mentoring sessions
- Technical risk assessments and mitigation plans
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Guide Developers on architectural decisions and best practices
- Advise Product Managers on technical feasibility and trade-offs
- Collaborate with QA Leads on testability and quality standards
- Support Project Managers by identifying technical dependencies and risks
- Consult with Executive Sponsors on significant technical investments

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove process blockers, and coach teams on agile practices. They help teams maintain sustainable velocity and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove impediments and blockers to team progress
- Coach team members on agile practices and mindset
- Maintain sprint health and team morale
- Track and report on team metrics and velocity
- Support retrospective action items

### Goals
- Maintain high team productivity and engagement
- Improve team processes through continuous feedback
- Foster psychological safety and open communication

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-one coaching with team members
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- Velocity and team health reporting

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Support Developers in overcoming impediments
- Facilitate planning with Product Managers and Project Managers
- Report team health and concerns to Project Managers
- Coach all team members on agile principles and collaboration

---

## Design / UX Lead

### Role Summary
Design/UX Leads own user experience strategy, interaction design, and usability standards. They ensure products are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with user needs.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and define user personas/journeys
- Define UX standards, patterns, and design systems
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Review product implementations for usability compliance
- Identify and advocate for accessibility improvements
- Collaborate on feature prioritization based on user impact

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible products that delight users
- Reduce support burden through excellent UX
- Establish consistent design patterns and brand experience

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and usability testing sessions
- User research findings and personas
- Design system documentation and component specs
- Feedback on product implementations

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partner with Product Managers on user-centered prioritization
- Collaborate with Developers on design implementation
- Work with QA Leads on usability testing
- Guide Technical Leads on UX-driven architectural needs
- Advocate to Executive Sponsors on user-centric value

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure projects meet security standards, regulatory requirements, and organizational policies. They identify security risks and guide secure implementation practices.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements for projects
- Conduct security reviews and threat assessments
- Establish secure development practices and standards
- Review third-party dependencies and vendor security
- Coordinate incident response and post-incident reviews
- Track and report on security and compliance metrics

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and compliance violations
- Build security into development practices from the start
- Minimize organizational risk and customer trust impact

### Typical Communication
- Security requirements and compliance checklists
- Security review findings and remediation guidance
- Incident response coordination and updates
- Security metrics and risk reporting

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Define security requirements with Product Managers and Technical Leads
- Review code changes with Developers and QA
- Advise Project Managers on security-related risks
- Report compliance status to Executive Sponsors
- Collaborate on secure architecture with Technical Leads
